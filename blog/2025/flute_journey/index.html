Classical Music Journey

Created on December 30, 2025

2025   ·   formatting   charts   ·   sample-posts

Hi guys! I was very much keen into classical music from the early childhood, right from listening to my parents and sister singing and learning bandishs during late 2000s. Mainly because of my parents forcing me to pickup a new hobby, I was put into Hindustani music vocal classes (every sunday) right from my 3rd standard (in contrary to all “boys” joining the tabla classes). Initially I did not like the same but later on I slowly started liking it.

After few days I started suddenly pickingup the relative scale with high precision - and started playing harmonium (the indian piano). That is when I realised that I can decode the corresponding notes of whatever is being played (basically very good at relative pitch in my opinion). So i slowly started attending “bhajans” to practice my harmonium skills and vocal skills. As a part of annual day function in my school and music classes, I was fortunate to be given a 10-15 minute slot to showcase my skills (very thankful to them to abolish my stage-fear 🙏)

Slowly my parents also pickedup my “fast” learning and insisted me to learn playing flute. I was in 5th standard back then. Early days were hard since I was feeling very breathless but later I got a hang of it after practicing for ~ 30 mins eveyrday. I remember my dad taking me on a scooty for 10kms one way every wednesday even when there used to be extreme rain (big shoutout 🫡) for the flute class. No wonder the vocals classes also complemented since the content was similar and more relaxed (due to no pressure of writing any exam unlike vocals class). Due to my skill of picking up the notes easily, I could eventually learn everything that is being taught with just a bottleneck to practice the movement of fingers for the flute.

Eventually I started performing everywhere in Bhatkal, playing in bhajans, functions, annual day etc and yeah, I used to get paid also sometimes (apparently this was my “first” salary and not internship money 😭), sometimes used to go to places which were couple of hundreds of kilometers away from my place. I used to collect this money and put into a box named “sitar money” since I was very fond of learning sitar. There wasn’t any smartphone/internet facility during my childhood, so I used to listen to these instruments in the “Insync” channel in out Television. Hopefully I’ll buy and learn sitar one day. This continued till 10th standard when I gave my Karnataka State Junior vocals examination and Gandharva examination till Praveshika Poorna and successfully qualified it.

Later due to JEE and covid, we shifted to Mangalore - making this come to an abrupt end. I used to practice the instruments sometimes but didn’t go for any functions since it would hinder my prep. I couldn’t give my Karnataka State Senior vocals examination due to similar constraints (my parents are always sad about this, they still ask me to give this exam someday lol, because my sister had also cleared the exam). Eventually after getting into IITB, I explored Roots club, participated in their jammings, Dharohar and other flagship events, meeting artists, playing harmonium, flute and singing as well. I also conducted an Summer school of Cult program where I would conduct flute classes - the resources are attached here - Flute_Class_Notes and SSOC26_Flute_Intro

These are some of my resources from vocals and flute class that I used to attend during my childhood. Can be helpful if you know Kannada language script.
