Hi, I am Parth Pai, an incoming Data Science Masters student at UCSD during Fall 2026. I graduated with a B.Tech degree in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Data Science from [IIT Bombay](https://www.iitb.ac.in) in Mumbai, India. I spent my Spring 2025 as an exchange student at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se) in Stockholm, Sweden. Previously I had completed my pre university education from [CFAL Mangalore](https://www.cfalindia.com). My research interest lie in the intersection of Machine Learning and Music.

I was fortunate to work in [Jaguar Land Rover](https://www.jlr.com/) as a Machine learning intern in their team during the Summers of 2025. I also worked in a computational neuroscience project during my stay at KTH optimizing the firing rates of neurons. Previously I was also a part of [IITB Student Satellite Program](https://www.aero.iitb.ac.in/satlab/) working in the Guidance, Navigation and Controls team.

I am trained professionally in Hindustani classical Flute and Harmonium. During my leisure time, I play these instruments and sing sometimes. You can check out my performances in [Roots IITB](https://www.youtube.com/@rootsiitbombay1493) and [Symphony IITB](https://www.youtube.com/@SymphonyIITBombay) youtube channels. I like to go on hikes and travel around to explore nature (mountains > beaches).
